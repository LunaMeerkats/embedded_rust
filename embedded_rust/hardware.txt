The board I am using is the NUCLEO-H753ZI.

The NUCLEO-H753ZI development board uses a 32-bit ARM Cortex-M7 core architecture 
(specifically the Cortex-M7F sub-architecture) via its on-board STM32H753ZI microcontroller.
